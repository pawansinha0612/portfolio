How to use it

  • Verdict first. PASS, PASS WITH RISKS or FAIL, then three sentences. Most readers stop there.
  • Percentiles from the steady state. Leave the ramp-up out of the numbers, and don't average percentiles across runs or servers. Why averages lie shows what goes wrong.
  • Every finding needs evidence. A graph link, a query or a trace ID. "The database looked slow" is an opinion.
  • Analyse before you write. The JTL analyser and k6 results viewer turn raw results into the key-numbers table.

Write the verdict first, in three sentences or fewer, for someone who reads nothing else. For example: "The release meets all response-time targets at 1.5 × last year's peak. Checkout errors reached 0.8% at peak, above the 0.5% target, because of payment gateway timeouts. Recommend go-live once the timeout fix is retested."
