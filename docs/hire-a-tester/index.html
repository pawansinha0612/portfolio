How it works

From brief to hire

  1. 01

    Send the brief

    The role, level, must-haves and salary range. Two minutes with the form below.

  2. 02

    Intake call

    20 minutes on the team, the product and how you interview, so I know what "good" means for you.

  3. 03

    Technical screen

    I interview each candidate on real testing problems for your stack, not keyword matching.

  4. 04

    Shortlist

    Up to 3 candidates, each with my written notes on strengths, gaps and what to probe.

  5. 05

    You interview and hire

    I handle scheduling and feedback. You pay only if you hire someone I introduced.

Fees: success-based, a percentage of first-year salary, quoted on the call and confirmed in writing before I send any CVs. Nothing up front. If a hire leaves early, I find a replacement at no extra cost (the period is in the terms).

Questions

Before you send a brief

Where do the candidates come from?

Testers in my network and my talent pool: people who use my interview prep, AI practice and jobs pages and asked to hear about roles. I only put someone forward after a technical interview, and only with their permission.

Permanent or contract?

Permanent roles first. For contract roles, mention it in the brief and I'll tell you straight away whether I can help.

Which locations?

Australia-wide: remote, hybrid or on-site. I'm based in Melbourne.

How fast?

It depends on the role and the market. I'll give you an honest timeline on the intake call rather than a number here.

Can you also test the candidates' take-home tasks?

Yes. I can design a short practical exercise for your stack and mark it, so your team only meets people who can do the work.
