Privacy

What happens to your details

  • What I collect: what you type in the form above. If you tick the practice box, a summary of your AI practice scores from this browser (number of answers, average and best score). Nothing else.
  • Why: only to match you with testing roles and contact you about them.
  • Where it's kept: a private Google Sheet that only I can open.
  • Who sees it: me. I never send your details or CV to a company without asking you first, and I never sell or share the list.
  • Deleting it: the confirmation email has a one-click delete link. Or email hello@pawansinha.com.au and I'll delete everything.
