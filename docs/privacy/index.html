Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, where it goes and how to get it deleted. Written in plain English. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Who I am

This site, pawansinha.com.au, is run by Pawan Sinha, a software testing and performance engineering consultant in Melbourne, Australia (ABN 43 627 514 346). Questions or requests about your information: hello@pawansinha.com.au.

I handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What you send me

I only collect what you type into a form or chat on this site:

  • Enquiries, chat messages and call requests: your name, email, company and message, so I can reply.
  • Email sign-ups (the weekly digest, the k6 email course, job alerts, the peak-readiness checklist and quiz report): your email address and the options you chose, so I can send what you asked for.
  • Talent pool and hiring briefs: the details you enter, used only to match testers with roles. I never pass a tester's details to a company without asking first.
  • Site speed check: the web address you enter and your email if you ask for the report.
  • AI interview practice: your typed or spoken answer is sent to an AI service (Google Gemini, or NVIDIA as a backup) to score it. Don't include personal or confidential details in answers.

These details are stored in a private Google Sheet that only I can open, using Google Apps Script. I don't sell, rent or share them, and I don't add you to any list you didn't sign up for. Every email I send has an unsubscribe link.

What stays on your computer

  • Free tools (the converters, results analysers, thread dump and GC log analysers, workload model and calculators) work entirely in your browser. Files and text you put into them are not uploaded or stored by me.
  • Browser storage: some pages remember things in your own browser (local storage): learning-path ticks, game best scores, practice history and chat state. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. I can't see it.

Visitor statistics

I count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites; I see totals such as page views, referring sites, browser type and country, not who you are.

Advertising

Some free content pages (guides, glossary, blog and tools) may show ads served by Google AdSense. Pages about my services do not.

  • Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on those visits.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads use cookies.

Other services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site; like any web host it processes your IP address to deliver pages.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries (cdnjs, jsDelivr) load fonts and scripts, which means your browser contacts their servers.
  • Calendly handles call bookings, under its own privacy policy, if you choose to book.
  • Questions under guides use giscus and GitHub Discussions. If you post one, it is public on GitHub under your GitHub account, and GitHub's privacy policy applies. The question box loads only when you scroll to it.
  • YouTube videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Gumroad processes purchases of my paid products; I receive your name, email and what you bought, never your card details.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

Your rights

  • Ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it: email hello@pawansinha.com.au and I'll act within 30 days. Talent pool sign-ups also get a one-click delete link.
  • If you're not happy with how I handled a request, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Children

This site is for working professionals and isn't directed at children under 16. I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
