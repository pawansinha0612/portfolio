What you get

Questions

Common questions

Is it too late before Black Friday?

Usually not. A focused test of your key journeys takes about a week. The sooner we talk, the more time you have to fix what it finds.

Will testing affect real customers?

We test a staging copy where possible, or production in a quiet window you choose, with a kill switch and you watching.

We're on Shopify, BigCommerce or a SaaS platform. Is it still worth it?

Yes. The platform scales, but your apps, custom code, search, payment and third-party scripts often don't. We test what you control and agree limits with the platform's terms.

What does it cost?

A fixed price, agreed after a free call, based on how many journeys and environments we test. No day-rate surprises.
