How to use this page

Turn the answers into your own stories

Don't memorise the wording. Interviewers can tell. Learn the structure of each answer, then swap in a system, numbers and tools from your own work.

Use STAR for "tell me about a time" questions: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Finish with the lesson. A good story takes 60 to 90 seconds to tell.

Only claim what you did. If you followed someone else's test plan, say so, and show you understand why it was written that way. Knowing it is not the same as having done it, and good interviewers can tell the difference.

Tick "Practised" on each question once you can answer it out loud without looking. Your ticks are saved in this browser only.

Then test yourself: practise out loud with instant feedback on a random question at your level.
