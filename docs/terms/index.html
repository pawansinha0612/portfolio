Terms of engagement

How I work with clients on testing and performance engineering projects. Written in plain English. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Who these terms are between

These terms apply when you engage Pawan Sinha (ABN 43 627 514 346, Melbourne, Australia) directly for testing or performance engineering work. If you hire me through Upwork, a recruitment agency or under your own contract, that platform's or contract's terms apply instead wherever they differ from these.

Quotes and scope

  • Before any work starts, I send a written quote or statement of work covering what's included, what isn't, the timeline and the price. Work starts once you accept it in writing (an email is fine).
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days.
  • If the scope changes, for example more user journeys or extra test rounds, I'll tell you the effect on price and timeline and only go ahead once you agree.

Fees and payment

  • Fixed-price work: 50% when you accept the quote and 50% on delivery of the final report, unless the quote says otherwise.
  • Hourly or daily work: invoiced every two weeks, based on time actually worked.
  • Invoices are payable by bank transfer within 14 days.
  • Reasonable pre-approved costs, such as cloud load generators beyond what the quote includes, are passed on at cost.

Rescheduling and cancellation

  • You can move a scheduled test date at no cost with 5 business days' notice.
  • If you cancel a project, you pay for work done up to that point. Any upfront payment above that amount is refunded.

What you're responsible for

  • Confirming that you own the systems to be tested or have permission to test them.
  • Telling your hosting or cloud provider, CDN, payment gateway and any other third parties about load tests where their terms require it.
  • Providing a suitable test environment, test data and accounts, and the access I need on time. Delays in access may move the delivery date.
  • Having backups and a way to restore the systems under test.

Load testing safety

Load and stress tests deliberately push systems hard, and can slow or stop them. To keep that under control, we agree the target environment, test windows, maximum load and a stop condition before any test runs, and I stop immediately if you ask. I'm not responsible for slowdowns or outages that happen while running tests within the agreed plan; finding those limits safely is the point of the work.

Confidentiality

I keep your code, data, systems and results confidential, use them only for your project, and delete or return them when the work ends if you ask. I'm happy to sign your NDA. I won't name you as a client or use your project as a case study without your written permission.

Who owns the work

  • Once you've paid in full, the test scripts, workload models, dashboards and reports made for your project are yours.
  • My pre-existing tools, templates and know-how stay mine. Where they're part of what I deliver, you get a permanent licence to use them for your own business.

Results

Test results show how your system behaved in the tested environment, at the tested load, at that time. I'll explain the risks I see clearly, but I can't guarantee how production will perform, because real traffic, data and infrastructure can differ.

Liability

  • My total liability for any engagement is limited to the fees you paid for it.
  • I'm not liable for indirect or consequential losses such as lost profits, revenue or data.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that can't be excluded.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. If something goes wrong, let's talk first; most problems are solved with a phone call.
