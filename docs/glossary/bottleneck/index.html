What is Bottleneck?

A bottleneck is the single resource or component that limits a system's throughput, so improving anything else makes little difference until it is fixed.

Why it matters

Teams often tune what they can see instead of what limits them. Adding pods when the database is the bottleneck costs money and can make things worse. There is always one, and after you fix it, the next one appears.

An example

Checkout tops out at 180 orders a minute. App CPU is 35%, but a payment provider allows 3 calls a second per merchant account, which is 180 a minute. No amount of app tuning helps. The fix is a rate limit increase or a second merchant account.

How to measure or test it

Increase load and find the first resource that saturates, then confirm with tracing or profiling where requests spend their time. Change one thing, retest and check that the limit moved.

Common mistakes

  • Fixing several things at once, so you don't know which helped.
  • Forgetting external limits such as third-party rate limits and licence caps.
